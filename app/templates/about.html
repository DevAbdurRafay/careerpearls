Our Core Pillars of Trust

How we protect talent and elevate genuine organizations across Pakistan

Rigorous Employer Verification

Every employer on CareerPearls undergoes a strict verification checklist including National Tax Number (NTN) validation, corporate email domain matching, and physical operating presence review before publishing jobs.

Anti-Ghosting Accountability

We believe candidates deserve transparency. Employers must take affirmative action on applications (Review, Shortlist, Schedule Interview, or Provide Status Update) within strict operational timelines.

Zero Tolerance for Scams & Fees

Any organization found asking job seekers for registration charges, processing fees, or offering misleading compensation is permanently blocked, blacklisted, and audited by our Trust & Safety team.

Direct Secure Communication

Built-in secure candidate-to-recruiter messaging and integrated interview scheduling eliminate third-party agency middlemen and empower direct talent discovery.
