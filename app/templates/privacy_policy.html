Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly during registration, profile creation, and job applications — including your full name, email address, phone number, resume documents, educational credentials, and employment history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to match you with relevant job opportunities, allow verified employers to review your applications (only when you actively submit an application), facilitate interview coordination, and improve our platform security and capabilities.

3. Data Sharing & Visibility

Your candidate profile and uploaded resumes are only made accessible to employers for job postings you explicitly apply to. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers.

4. Cookies & Storage

We use secure session cookies for user authentication and local storage for theme and UI preferences. We do not use third-party behavioral trackers.

5. Your Data Rights

You have full control over your data. You may update your profile details, delete uploaded resumes, or request account deactivation and complete data erasure at any time directly from your account settings.

6. Security Measures

All passwords are encrypted using strong cryptographic hashing algorithms. All data in transit is protected using TLS/HTTPS. We enforce CSRF tokens, strict role-based access control, and comprehensive audit logging.

7. Contact Support

For privacy inquiries or data protection requests, contact our support team at career.pulse.web@gmail.com.
