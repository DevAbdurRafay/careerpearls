Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide during registration, profile creation, and job applications — including name, email, phone, resume, and work history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to match you with relevant job opportunities, allow employers to review your applications (only when you apply), and improve our platform features.

3. Data Sharing

Your profile is only shared with employers when you actively apply to their listings. We never sell personal data to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use session cookies for authentication and local storage for preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Your Rights

You may request account deletion and data removal at any time from your profile settings.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed. All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. We implement CSRF protection and secure session management.

7. Contact

Privacy inquiries: career.pulse.web@gmail.com
